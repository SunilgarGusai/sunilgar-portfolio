Academic Identity

Mathematics, structure, networks and computation.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Faculty of Computer Applications at Marwadi University, Rajkot, working across mathematics, computing and data-oriented programmes. My academic work is built on a strong mathematical foundation and increasingly emphasizes reproducible computational research.

My doctoral and continuing research centre on spectral graph theory, graph energy, eccentricity-based graph invariants and their applications. A central strand of this work is Vertex Eccentricity Labeled Energy (VELE), which I study both theoretically and as a structural descriptor for complex networks.

I am developing a broader research programme connecting rigorous graph-theoretic ideas with network resilience, scientific machine learning, reliable predictive modelling, QSPR and graph-based computational methods.

Current research directions

VELE & Spectral Invariants

Structural theory, graph operations, spectral bounds and extensions of eccentricity-driven energy measures.

Network Resilience

Structural screening of vulnerable nodes and edges, including validation against physically meaningful network behaviour.

Explainable Graph-Based Screening

Mathematically interpretable methods for identifying influential or failure-sensitive graph components before disruption.

Inverse Spectral Problems

Reconstruction questions for symmetric matrices and graphs using structured spectral information.

Reliable Scientific ML

Uncertainty quantification, distribution shift, grouped validation and evaluation protocols designed to reduce leakage and overclaiming.

QSPR & Molecular Graphs

Graph-theoretic and spectral descriptors for quantitative structure–property modelling and mathematical chemistry.
